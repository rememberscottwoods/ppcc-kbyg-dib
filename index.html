Before you go

Arrive with a mission question

The strongest conversations start with a deployment boundary, a control requirement, or an operational outcome—not a generic product tour.

01 · Frame

Bring one production scenario

  • Who acts, on what data, and with which identity?
  • What must remain private, isolated, or auditable?
  • What outcome would prove mission value?
02 · Map

Know your boundaries

  • Tenant and environment strategy
  • DLP, connectors, endpoints, and network paths
  • ALM, source control, monitoring, and support ownership
03 · Prepare

Pack for a working conference

  • Comfortable shoes and a layer for cold rooms
  • Phone charger or battery pack
  • Whova app, backup session choices, and meeting notes

Mission plan

Three days, three decisions

Use the conference to leave with a clearer operating model, a defensible architecture, and a next production move. This is a decision path—not a fabricated session timetable.

Day 1 · Govern

Define the control plane

  1. Start with a government or agent-governance roundtable.
  2. Compare environment, DLP, identity, and tool governance patterns.
  3. Capture three controls your current operating model lacks.
Day 2 · Architect

Trace the trusted path

  1. Prioritize managed identity, VNet, Dataverse security, and OneLake controls.
  2. Ask how telemetry and audit evidence cross service boundaries.
  3. Sketch a reference path from user to action to data.
Day 3 · Operationalize

Move one workload forward

  1. Focus on ALM, observability, production readiness, and field resilience.
  2. Identify the owner for operations, adoption, and measurable value.
  3. Leave with one 30-day experiment and a clear stop/go gate.

FAQ & links

Confirm the source of truth

This guide is a curated lens. The official conference site and Whova remain authoritative for session changes, rooms, registration, and event policies.

Is every listed session explicitly about the DIB?

No. Four sessions are directly government-focused; the rest were selected because their controls and operating patterns are highly relevant to regulated defense environments.

Does a session's inclusion imply GCC High availability?

No. Session descriptions discuss product patterns, not tenant-specific feature commitments. Validate availability, licensing, compliance scope, and roadmap claims for your exact cloud before making an architecture decision.

Why are exact session times not shown?

The public session catalog provides titles, descriptions, and speakers, while final time and room details may change. Use Whova as the live agenda.

What should I ask at the Microsoft booth?

Bring a precise scenario and ask for the supported identity flow, data boundary, network path, telemetry model, ALM pattern, and cloud-specific feature availability.
